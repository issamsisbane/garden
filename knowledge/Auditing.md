# Auditing

## What is auditing ?

**Track activities** : Auditing is tracking all changes useful to troubleshoot and monitor and knowing all these questions about requests on the api-server : 
- Who ?
- When ?
- What ?
- How ?

## Why it is important

**Compliance** : It also ensure compliance with standards like : 
- GDPR
- HIPAA
- PCI-DSS

**Understand incidents** : It provides a detailed trail that helps investigate and understand the scope and impact of the incident.
