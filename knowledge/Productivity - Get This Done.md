
## Principles 

- Everything go first in the inbox to get it of the head quickly
- Not hesitate to Prune task we will never do 

Structure your power block:

- First 5 minutes: Review where you left off, load context
- Next 75 minutes: Focused building
- Final 10 minutes: Document stopping point, capture next steps

[[Zeigarnik Effect]] spend just 10 minutes on your project.
## Tasks Types

- coding => Better the moring
- documentation
- review => Better the afternoon
- messages / mails

## Tasks Tags

### Priority

- `#now`
- `#next`
- `#later`
### State

- `#someday-maybe` : Nice to have tasks
- `#office` — tasks requiring your work setup
- `#home` — personal errands and tasks
- `#deepwork` — tasks requiring focused concentration
- `#quick` — 5-minute tasks for low-energy moments
- `#waiting-for` — delegated or blocked items
## Daily Review Checklist

- Start the day by choosing the tasks of the day
- Reorganizing tasks
## Weekly Review Checklist

- **Clear inbox to zero** — Process every item  
- **Review all projects** — Is there a next action for each?  
- **Check Waiting For** — Follow up on delegated items  
- **Review Someday/Maybe** — Promote or prune items  
- **Review upcoming calendar** — Prepare for commitments  
- **Update priorities** — What matters most this week? Wins of the week
